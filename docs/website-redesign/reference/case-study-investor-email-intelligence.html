    In a nutshell

    The client manages relationships across a global network of investors, partners and companies, and most of that relationship lives in email. Analysts were reading every message and retyping it into the CRM. We built a multi-agent system on Anthropic Claude that ingests each email, extracts the people and organisations, works out what the message means, and writes a validated CRM record linked to its source.

    Client data protectedThe client is anonymised, and every email, investor and figure in the demos is fictional. The workflow is real; the data is not.
    01 · The problem

    The signals were there. Buried in the inbox.

    Every week brought hundreds of emails carrying intros, commitments, declines and requests. Each one had to be read and retyped by an analyst. Set the weekly volume and watch the queue.

    Inbox · waiting for an analyst
    Manual entry
    With the pipeline
    analyst hours back every week

    Illustrative: assumes about 12 minutes of analyst time per email, reduced by the measured 90%.

    What was going wrong
    • Analyst time sunk in typing: reading, interpreting and re-entering every message by hand.
    • A CRM that lagged: records were updated days after the conversation happened.
    • Inconsistent capture: two analysts recorded the same kind of email in two different ways.
    • No view of engagement: nobody could see which investors were warming up or cooling off.
    02 · The pipeline

    Five agents. One email in, one clean record out.

    Pick an email and run it through ingestion, extraction, classification, structuring and validation. Some emails make one record, some make three, and some rightly make none.

    Fictional emails and investors. The stages are the real pipeline.
    03 · Traceability

    Every field points back to its sentence

    Audit was a hard requirement, so nothing in the CRM floats free of its evidence. Click any field in the record to see exactly where it came from.

    How the data stays trustworthy
    • Linked to the source: every record stores the message it came from, so anyone can open the original.
    • Evidence per field: extracted values keep the passage that supports them.
    • Validated before writing: required fields, known organisations and duplicates are checked first.
    • Same rules every time: one schema for every email, whoever would once have typed it.
    04 · Engagement intelligence

    From emails to engagement trends

    Once every email is a structured record, relationships become measurable. See who is warming up, who is cooling off, and the conversations behind each trend.

    Illustrative dashboard with fictional investors.
    05 · Architecture

    A pipeline that runs itself

    n8n captures the mail, a FastAPI service coordinates the Claude agents, and PostgreSQL keeps every record with its source and audit trail, all in Docker containers in the cloud. Tap a component, or trace an email.

    06 · What made it hard

    Messy inboxes, strict audit

    Real investor email is informal, multi-topic and inconsistent, and the output had to stand up to audit. Flip each card from the challenge to how we solved it.

    07 · The result

    A manual workflow became an intelligence pipeline

    Following production deployment, the firm processes its investor email automatically and sees its relationships in data, not just in inboxes.

    300–500investor emails processed every week
    90%reduction in manual processing effort
    70+analyst hours saved every week
    1source email linked to every CRM record
    The resultConsistent, auditable CRM data generated from every investor conversation, and a live view of engagement that lets the firm manage relationships and make decisions faster.
