    In a nutshell

    The client's AI chatbot lets more than 80 million users reach its services by voice, chat and image. It was built for experimentation, then adoption exploded: rate-limit errors, failed requests, slow answers outside one region, and no clear view of what was going wrong. We built the layer that manages every request before it reaches a model provider: a global queue, intelligent routing across providers, multi-region deployment, full tracing, and recovery when anything fails.

    Client data protectedThe client is anonymised, and every traffic figure, latency and request in the demos is illustrative. The architecture is real; the data is not.
    01 · The problem

    Built for experiments, hit by 80 million users

    Every provider caps how many requests it accepts. With no layer to manage traffic, anything over the cap came back as an HTTP 429 error, and the user got nothing. Push the traffic up and compare.

    What was going wrong
    • Rate-limit failures: traffic above a provider's limit failed outright.
    • No routing: requests could not move to another provider when one was full.
    • One region: users elsewhere in the world waited longer for every answer.
    • Flying blind: engineers had little insight into where requests failed.
    • Spikes broke things: sudden surges made the whole system unstable.
    • A ceiling on growth: the platform could not scale to enterprise usage.
