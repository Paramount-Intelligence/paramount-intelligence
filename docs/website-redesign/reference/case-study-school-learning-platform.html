    In a nutshell

    A fully remote early-years school that families anywhere in the world can join, with live interactive classes and character building at its heart. We built its learning platform as a full school operating system: six role-based portals that run admissions, finance and live teaching as one pipeline, plus smart visual learning and AI-made lesson videos.

    02 · The problem

    A school run on a patchwork

    A school with no campus runs entirely on its systems, and these lived in different tools. Ask a simple question and nobody could see the whole picture.

    Ask the school a question
    ONE CONNECTED RECORD

    Tap a question

    What was going wrong
    • Leaks in the admissions funnel: prospects went cold because follow-up was manual and inconsistent.
    • No single source of truth: a child's interview, admission form, fees and enrolment lived in four different places.
    • Fragile finance: monthly fees, discounts, scholarships and dues were reconciled by hand, and access was rarely enforced.
    • Invisible teaching: nothing recorded whether a lecture happened, who attended, or where the recording was.
    The needCollapse the whole stack into one auditable, role-aware platform where every step is a record, every record is connected, and repetitive work is automated.
    03 · The resolution

    Six portals, one codebase

    Each role gets its own product: its own menu, pages and permissions. Pick a role to see what they see.

    lms.school.example/coordinator
    Real screen · names and emails blurred
    How access is enforced
    • Proxy guard: sends signed-out users to login, everyone else to their own dashboard, and blocks cross-role pages.
    • Role checks on every API: each sensitive endpoint confirms the caller's role before acting.
    • Business rules on top: coordinators can't exceed the discount cap; only unpaid or rejected vouchers accept new proof.
    • One login, many roles: sign in with email, phone or username; staff with several roles choose one at login.
    • Parents see their own children: the parent portal shows only their own children's lectures, homework, attendance and fees.
    • Teachers stay in their portal: teachers work in the teacher portal only and cannot open parent or student portals.
    One family's journey through the platform

    One record carries a family from first enquiry to live class. Each step is enforced in code, logged to the audit trail and sent to the right people by email or WhatsApp.

    Fees that reconcile themselves

    Build a voucher, then follow a family's fees month to month. Unpaid balances carry forward, and the classroom locks when a fee is overdue.

    How the money side works
    • One formula, protected: total = (regular fee + admission fee) − discount − scholarship, never below zero, itemised line by line.
    • A discount cap for coordinators: set in fee settings (20% by default); admins can go further.
    • Monthly batches: one voucher per enrolled student per class, numbered LMS-YYYYMM-####, emailed with a payment link.
    • Carry-forward and gating: remaining = carried-in dues + this month − paid; an overdue monthly voucher pauses LMS access.
    06 · Payment verification

    Four ways in, one approval engine

    However a payment is confirmed, it goes through the same verification logic. Pick a path and watch what one approval sets up.

    Approving an admission payment
    Verification engineapprove · reject
    Why one engine matters
    • Four entry paths: parent upload on the public page, coordinator approval on a parent's behalf, scholarship approval, and a voucher-level fallback.
    • One outcome: every path ends in the same approval, so accounts and fee history stay consistent.
    • Accounts on approval: parent and student accounts, enrolment, a roll number and login details, sent to the parent by email.
    • Rejections explained: a rejected payment emails the reason and a link to resubmit; every decision is audited.
    From a monthly fee to restored access
    1. 01Fee calculatedThe voucher is itemised from the regular fee, less any discount or scholarship, and emailed with a payment link.
    2. 02Proof submittedThe parent uploads payment proof. Only an unpaid or rejected voucher accepts it.
    3. 03ReviewedA coordinator approves or rejects it through the verification engine. A rejection emails the reason and a link to resubmit.
    4. 04ApprovedThe voucher is marked verified, the fee history and carry-forward are updated, and an approval email is sent.

    Access: when a monthly voucher is overdue, LMS access stays paused while it is unpaid, submitted or rejected. It returns automatically the moment the payment is approved.

    Why the AI layer is credible
    • It reuses existing data: lead signals, interview answers, Meet recordings, fee history and attendance already exist.
    • It reuses existing infrastructure: pgvector on the current Postgres; recordings already land in Drive.
    • Humans stay in the loop: payments, grades, admissions and messages are drafts or scores a person confirms.

    10 · Architecture

    Five layers, one request path

    Pick a journey and trace it through the system, from the screen a family taps to the services working behind it.

      Design choices
      • One Next.js codebase: six dashboards, the public flows and around 180 API routes, guarded at the edge and again on every API.
      • A hybrid data layer: Prisma plus raw SQL on PostgreSQL; files sit in private storage buckets and are opened with signed links.
      • Google Workspace by service account: Calendar, Meet, Admin Reports and Drive work without anyone's personal login.
      • Built for any country: country and city lists come from public APIs, and email and WhatsApp reach families wherever they live.
