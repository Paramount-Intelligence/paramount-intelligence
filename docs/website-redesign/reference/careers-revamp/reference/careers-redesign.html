Our hiring process

A structured, transparent process designed to assess capability, alignment, and long-term potential.

  1. 1

    Application review

    We review your background, experience, and problem-solving exposure, then hold a focused conversation about your goals and expectations, and give you clarity on our work, standards, and environment.

  2. 2

    Practical or technical assessment

    Depending on the role, you complete a structured assessment based on real-world scenarios across AI, data, cloud, automation, or platform engineering. We look for ownership, judgment, and clear communication.

  3. 3

    Technical or strategy interview

    A deep discussion with senior engineers or consultants on structured thinking, technical depth, and problem-solving approach. You also meet potential team members and discuss how you collaborate.

  4. 4

    Final decision

    We provide clear feedback at the end of the process and extend an offer if there is strong mutual alignment. We then agree the role, team, and start details with you.

The role

AI Engineer, Applied AI team.

What the role will work on

AI agents, retrieval, evaluation, and enterprise AI applications delivered with client teams.

Responsibilities

  • Design and build AI agents, copilots, and retrieval workflows connected to client systems.
  • Define evaluation sets and quality checks, and use the results to guide each release.
  • Work directly with client users and technical owners from discovery through deployment.
  • Integrate models and tools with APIs, data sources, permissions, and monitoring.
  • Document decisions and hand over systems that client teams can operate and extend.

Relevant experience

  • Production experience with LLM applications, retrieval-augmented generation, or agent frameworks.
  • Strong Python, plus experience with APIs and cloud services such as AWS.
  • Experience evaluating model outputs and handling failure cases and uncertainty.
  • Clear communication with both technical colleagues and business users.

Location and working hours

Remote, US only. Working hours overlap with client teams as agreed for each engagement.
