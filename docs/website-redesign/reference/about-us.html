Founder & CEO

Ali Azzam

Ali leads technical direction, architecture, and delivery quality at Paramount. He has designed and shipped production AI systems for Fortune 500, PE-backed, and high-growth companies as well as funded startups, working directly with founders, executives, and engineering teams.

His experience spans AI agents and multi-agent platforms, enterprise copilots and retrieval, customer-facing AI for a telecom operator with 80M+ subscribers, data engineering and analytics, AWS cloud infrastructure, and workflow automation. He stays hands-on in technical decisions on every engagement.

Founder & Commercial Lead

Marty Kaufman

Marty leads client relationships and commercial alignment at Paramount. He brings commercial and executive leadership experience across technology services and is based in the United States, working with client executives to clarify priorities and shape each engagement.

He coordinates commercial decisions with Paramount's technical leadership, giving clients a clear point of contact for engagement structure, priorities, and ongoing collaboration. His focus is keeping delivery connected to the business result the client set out to achieve.
