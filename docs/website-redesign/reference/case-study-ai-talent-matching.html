    In a nutshell

    The client connects organisations with specialist consultants across industries and geographies. As the talent pool grew, staffing teams were still matching people to projects by hand, so shortlists were slow, outcomes depended on who did the matching, and good placements were missed. We built AI agents that parse project requirements, interpret consultant profiles, score fit with structured logic and produce ranked, explained shortlists, as decision support for staffing teams who keep the final say.

    Client data protectedThe client is anonymised, and every consultant, project, score and profile in the demos is fictional. The matching logic is real; the data is not.
    01 · The problem

    Keywords miss the best people

    Staffing teams searched and screened by hand, and a keyword search only finds the words you type. The project treated matching as a question of meaning instead. Run the same brief both ways.

    Project brief · fictional"Revenue forecasting lead for a fast-growing SaaS business"
    Fictional consultants. Illustrates the difference between keyword and semantic matching.
    What was going wrong
    • Judgment-dependent: matching relied heavily on each person's knowledge of the pool.
    • Slow screening: comparing consultants with requirements took significant time.
    • Did not scale: the bigger the talent pool, the slower staffing became.
    • Inconsistent: different teams produced different shortlists for similar roles.
    • Missed placements: some opportunities were lost while shortlists were built.
    • Under-used talent: available consultants went unnoticed, lowering utilisation.
    02 · Semantic role understanding

    A messy brief becomes a clear requirement

    Project descriptions arrive as free text. A role-parsing agent normalises each one into a structured representation the system can compare. Pick a brief, parse it, then click any field to see where it came from.

    Fictional briefs.
    03 · Talent profile interpretation

    Every CV, made comparable

    Consultants describe the same skill a dozen ways. A profile agent reads experience, skills and availability and converts them into one comparable profile. Watch the raw phrases resolve.

    Fictional consultants and profiles.
    04 · Structured candidate scoring

    A ranked shortlist, with the reasons attached

    Matching logic scores each consultant on skills, experience, industry and availability together, then ranks them. Change what matters for this project and watch the order move. Open any consultant to see why.

    Project · fictionalERP finance transformation · 6 months · EU time zones
    Fictional consultants, weights and scores. The structure (criteria, scores, ranking, explanations) mirrors the real system.
    05 · Human in the loop

    AI recommends. Staffing teams decide.

    The agents are a decision-support layer. Staffing teams review every recommendation and finalise placements, and because everyone starts from the same explained ranking, outcomes stop depending on who picked up the brief.

    Illustrative of the consistency gain. Fictional consultants.
    06 · Architecture

    Agents inside the staffing workflow

    Agent-based workflows connect to the client's staffing systems and data: project intake in, parsed requirements and interpreted profiles in the middle, an explained shortlist back to the team. Tap a component, or trace a project.

    07 · What made it hard

    Trust matters as much as speed

    Unstructured text on both sides, skills, experience and availability to weigh together, and a hard requirement that recommendations stay transparent and staffing teams stay in control. Flip each card.

    08 · The result

    A manual process became a scalable decision system

    Following deployment, the matching system improved staffing efficiency and placement speed, and increased utilisation across a global talent pool.

    ~25%improvement in staffing operational efficiency
    Fasterturnaround from role intake to candidate shortlist
    Lessmanual screening effort for staffing teams
    Consistenttalent matching decisions across teams
    The resultCandidate discovery accelerated, placements became more consistent, and more of the available talent pool was put to work, with staffing teams firmly in control of every decision.
