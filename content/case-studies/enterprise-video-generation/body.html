    In a nutshell

    Every new product launch or campaign needed a creative agency, so each video ad took weeks and cost a lot, and teams could only afford a few. We designed and built an autonomous video generation platform on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. A coordinated network of AI models researches the product, writes the script, produces the visuals, synthesises the voiceover and assembles the final ad. Production went from weeks to under 5 minutes, with over $1M in cost savings.

    What was going wrong
    • Slow production: every video required weeks of agency collaboration.
    • High cost per ad: production costs limited how many creative variations a campaign could have.
    • Slow testing: campaign testing and iteration could not keep up.
    • No personalisation at scale: ads could not be tailored across regions and audiences.
    • AI moving fast: new AI tools kept emerging, but needed a stable enterprise platform to be useful.
    • The need: professional, platform-ready video ads from simple business inputs, connected to the existing marketing systems.
    02 · The complication

    Five requirements, held at the same time

    Generating one video with AI is easy. Doing it as an enterprise platform is not. Tap each requirement to lock it in.

    Why each one mattered
    • Oracle Cloud: the platform had to meet enterprise security, scalability and data governance requirements.
    • Many AI services: no single model does script, imagery, video and voice well, so several had to work as one.
    • Languages and markets: campaigns run worldwide, so voice and copy had to be produced for each region.
    • Rendering at scale: a large portfolio of brands means many ads at once, not one at a time.
    • New models: AI tools change fast; the platform had to take in better models without a rebuild.
    03 · The resolution

    From simple inputs to a finished ad, automatically

    The platform runs the whole creative workflow itself. Press play, or open any stage.

    Illustration with a sample brief, script and copy. Scene stills are taken from the sample ad clips further down.

    What each stage does
    • AI research and content generation: large language models analyse product information and business inputs, then write the script and campaign messaging.
    • Multi-model video and image generation: specialised generative models produce the scenes, product imagery and supporting assets, chosen by the type of content needed.
    • Voice and audio synthesis: natural voiceovers, including localised audio for different regions (Eleven Labs among the voice tools).
    • Programmatic video rendering: visuals, voiceover and text overlays are assembled by server-side rendering pipelines into the finished ad.

    Why many models beat one
    • Specialists, not a generalist: each stage uses a model built for that kind of content.
    • Chosen per request: the system selects models dynamically for video, image, voice and rendering.
    • Built to adopt new models: the architecture takes in better models as the technology moves, without changing the workflow.
    • One coordinated system: marketers see one workflow, not a stack of separate AI tools.
