Our approach

Forward-deployed AI engineering

We work directly with the people who use the system and the teams responsible for running it. Our engineers help frame the problem, prototype the approach, connect the application to the client environment, and carry the agreed work through testing and release.

Engagement models

Ways to engage

A managed project or workstream

Paramount owns an agreed scope, with a named lead and acceptance measures.

A forward-deployed engineer or specialist

A Paramount engineer contributes directly to a defined client priority, with technical support and oversight agreed for the assignment.

An ongoing engineering partnership

Continuing development across an agreed roadmap, with defined capacity and a regular review cadence.

An advisory engagement

Focused assessment, evaluation, architecture, or delivery planning to support a specific decision.

Operational support

Maintain and improve an agreed system within defined coverage and responsibilities.

Accountability

Paramount remains the accountable contracting partner. Scope, delivery ownership, and ways of working are agreed before the engagement begins.
