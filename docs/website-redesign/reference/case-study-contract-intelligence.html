    In a nutshell

    Built to speed up the integration of two life sciences businesses after an $18 billion carve-out merger. Teams upload a contract and get a structured, clause-by-clause review from Claude in minutes, with no contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform needed.

    01 · The problem

    Review that doesn't scale

    Contracts arrive faster than people can read them, and risks slip through.

    The life of one contract
    Day 0ReceivedDays 1–3Legal reviewDay 3SignedMonth 10Opt-out windowMonth 12Renewal

    Drag the timeline, or let it play.

    What was going wrong
    • Contracts never stop coming: SOWs, MSAs and NDAs arrive constantly, and each must be checked before anyone signs.
    • Manual review takes days: a lawyer reads every page, clause by clause. Slow and expensive.
    • Results are inconsistent: different reviewers apply the standards unevenly.
    • Missed risks surface too late: a liability gap or missing renewal opt-out becomes a problem only after signing.
    The needEvery contract checked against the client's standards (liability caps, IP ownership, exit terms) before anyone signs.
    02 · The complication

    Six requirements, all at once

    Each requirement, written as a clause. Tap one to initial it.

    SCHEDULE A · REQUIREMENTSContract Intelligence Copilot
    Accepted for Phase 1
    0 of 6 held
    Why each one mattered
    • Real-world files: contracts arrive as PDF, DOCX or TXT, so all three had to work out of the box. Text-based PDFs work today. Scanned contracts need OCR, which is planned for Phase 3.
    • Long documents: vendor proposals can approach 200 pages and still need a full read.
    • Structured output: reviewers need statuses, fields and fixes they can act on, not free-form AI prose.
    • Secure handling: enterprise contracts stay encrypted, with credentials kept out of the code.
    • Fully serverless: nothing for the client to run or patch, and it scales on demand.
    • Long-running analysis: big reviews take longer than a normal web request, so processing runs asynchronously, with real-time chat on top.

    What the comparison returns
    • A side-by-side winner table: who is stronger on each clause, with a risk level per row.
    • A negotiation tip on every row, plus an overall recommendation and the top 3 things to push back on.
    • Chat built into the same screen: any question about either contract, answered with the actual clause cited.
    • One click to a draft negotiation letter for the weaker vendor, ready for legal to review.
    • A verdict on terms, not on the vendor: the recommendation says whose contract terms are stronger. It does not assess the vendors themselves, so choosing one stays with the team.
    • 10 clauses in the standard, 25 in the real comparison: the Validator checks one contract against the client's 10-clause standard. The comparison weighs two contracts against each other, clause by clause, so it is not limited to those ten: the real one under Proof covers 25 clauses.
    One contract, from upload to decision
    1. 1Upload. The vendor's contract goes into the document library once, as a PDF, DOCX or TXT file.
    2. 2Validate. Claude extracts the key terms and rates every clause against the client's 10-clause standard: GREEN, AMBER or RED, with a fix for each flagged clause.
    3. 3Triage. Procurement flags the high-risk items, so legal reads the flagged clauses, not every page.
    4. 4Ask. Reviewers question any clause in the AI Chat and get answers that cite the clause.
    5. 5Push back. The drafted follow-up questions go to the vendor. For two competing vendors, the comparison adds the top 3 points to negotiate and a draft negotiation letter for legal to review.
    6. 6Decide. The client's team decides whether to sign or go back to the vendor, using the report. The prototype does not route approvals: approval routing and workflow tracking are listed under next steps.

    Example from the demos above: §14.1 auto-renews with no opt-out window, so it is rated RED. The fix is a 60-day opt-out. The vendor answers the drafted questions, and the contract is signed after that one change.

    How the platform grows
    • Phase 1 (built): CloudFront, API Gateway, Lambda with FastAPI, Claude, encrypted S3 and Secrets Manager. Redeployable with AWS CDK in hours.
    • Phase 2 (planned): Azure AD single sign-on, a Lambda authorizer, a DynamoDB access list, 5-minute presigned URLs, RDS PostgreSQL, and CloudTrail auditing of every access attempt.
    • Phase 3 (planned): ECS Fargate (2–10 tasks), Multi-AZ RDS, ElastiCache, SQS and Textract OCR, with GuardDuty, Security Hub, Macie and blue/green deployments.
    • No one touches S3 directly: documents are served only through short-lived presigned links, and every attempt, granted or denied, is logged.
