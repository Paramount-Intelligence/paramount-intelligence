    In a nutshell

    Toptal engaged Paramount to design and build a production-grade shopping assistant as part of an agentic ecommerce initiative on AWS Bedrock AgentCore, focused on camping and outdoor gear. Customers describe what they need in their own words; the multi-agent assistant searches a live catalog, compares with context, manages the cart and checks out, with memory that persists across every turn and every visit.

    Browse, hesitate, leave.

    Customers know the goal, not the product. Keyword search only hears a word or two of what they say.

    What was going wrong
    • They know the goal, not the product: customers arrive to solve something (a trip, a home office) without knowing what to buy.
    • Search returns a wall of options: one keyword brings back hundreds of results, with no guidance on which is right.
    • Keyword search can't converse: it can't take in context or guide a customer from a need to a decision.
    • Assistants forgot everything: existing chat tools lost context between steps and couldn't run a cart or a checkout.
    The needGuide each customer from “I have a need” to “I have the right product”, in their own words.
    02 · The complication

    A conversation that never loses its place

    AgentCore can route turns of the same conversation to different runtime containers. State kept only in one container's memory is not there when a turn lands in another.

    Five turns, three containers

    Container B
    Container C
    Persistent state, outside the containersNot used
    S3Conversation history
    DynamoDBSession: trip, budget, results
    DynamoDBCart
    DynamoDBCustomer profile

    Illustration. When a turn lands on a different container, only state stored outside it carries over.

    What had to hold in production
    • State outside the process: session, cart, history and profile persist in DynamoDB and S3, so any container can pick up a turn, and the conversation carries over through recovery and later visits.
    • Structured output for the screen: every reply carries text plus signals that tell the React UI which panel to draw.
    • Relevant retrieval: catalog search had to stay accurate across very different ways of asking, with a Knowledge Base fallback.
    • Deterministic money moves: cart and checkout run through tools with fixed contracts, not free-form model output.

    How it's put together
    • One orchestrator, many tools: Claude decides what to do each turn and calls research, profile, cart and panel tools.
    • Retrieval-augmented search: a Bedrock Knowledge Base with Titan embeddings and S3 Vectors backs up catalog search.
    • Text plus structure: each reply is a ChatResponse with the message, the panel to show, products, cart and context pills.
    • Not a recommendation widget, not a scripted bot: it reasons, searches, compares, carts and checks out, across any catalog.

    What happened in this conversation
    • Context from one sentence: “August” and “Yellowstone” became pills with no form or dropdown, and the agent knew August means afternoon rain.
    • Two options, not hundreds, with the better pick flagged; the comparison used only weight, capacity, season and best use.
    • The cart moved with the conversation: “add two more tents” updated quantity, pricing and the live budget tracker at once.
    • One last useful pick: water purification tablets, flagged as essential for backcountry safety, added with a single “sure”.
    Confirmed orderCheckout runs through the deterministic checkout tool, not the model: it totals the cart held in DynamoDB, applies the bundle and loyalty discounts and confirms the order. The purchase is then added to the customer’s profile.
    05 · Returning customers

    Recognised, not reset.

    Most stores greet a loyal customer like a stranger. This agent loads who they are from the first message, and rewards them at checkout.

    Second visit, first message
    How recognition works
    • A persistent profile: location, group size, season, experience level and past purchases, kept in DynamoDB.
    • Loaded from the first message, so the agent can say “welcome back” and pick up where the last trip left off.
    • Rewarded at checkout: a 10% bundle discount and a 5% loyalty discount appear as clear line items, nothing to ask for.
    • Updated after every order: new purchases are added to the profile, so the next visit starts even further ahead.

    06 · What makes it different

    Not search. An advisor.

    When there's a smarter choice for the customer's situation, the agent says so. It doesn't wait to be asked. Tap a card to flip it.

    Why it stands apart
    • Production-grade multi-agent orchestration on AWS Bedrock AgentCore, not a prototype chatbot.
    • Structured UI signals travel with every conversational reply, so the storefront stays rich and in sync.
    • Distributed session resilience: memory survives containers, turns and visits.
    • Deterministic cart and checkout wired into the AI's reasoning, so the money side is always exact.
