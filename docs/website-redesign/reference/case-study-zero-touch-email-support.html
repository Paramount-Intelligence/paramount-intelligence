    Customer emails, answered with zero touch.

    A fully automated pipeline that reads, classifies, answers and routes customer emails around the clock for a national telecom provider, and hands a person only the cases that genuinely need one.

    • ~$300K a year saved: in recurring support operating costs.
    • Zero touch for most emails: routine queries resolved with no manual triage.
    • Safe by design: complex, sensitive or uncertain cases always go to an agent.

    In a nutshell

    The client runs one of the region's largest telecom and digital ecosystems, with millions of customer interactions a year. Every support email about billing, service issues, complaints or general questions had to be read, classified, answered and routed by a person. We built a zero-touch system that does all of that automatically in real time, combining deterministic workflow logic with NLP classification and policy-aligned responses, and escalates to agents only when a human is genuinely needed.

    Client data protectedThe client is anonymised, and every customer, email, number and volume in the demos is fictional, with personal details masked. The workflow is real; the data is not.
    01 · The problem

    Four manual steps for every email

    Read it, classify it, draft a reply, route the ticket: every email went through people, so at peak times the queue simply grew. Set the peak and see what a day looked like, then switch on zero touch.

    What was going wrong
    • High cost: whole support roles existed just to triage and send basic replies.
    • Slow first responses: customers waited, longest during busy periods.
    • Inconsistent answers: the same question got different replies from different agents.
    • Peak bottlenecks: volume spikes created backlogs and long delays.
    • Wasted skill: experienced agents spent their time on routine emails.
    02 · Ingestion and intent classification

    Every email understood in seconds

    Emails are captured straight from the enterprise mailbox. NLP classification decides what each one is about and how urgent it is, and flags anything sensitive. Pick an email and classify it.

    Automate the routine. Escalate the rest.

    05 · Workflow orchestration

    One workflow from inbox to resolution

    n8n orchestrates the whole process on AWS, connected to Google Workspace mail and the existing ticketing and support workflow. Send an email through it, or tap any node.

    06 · What made it hard

    Full automation, without the risk

    Answers had to be right, escalation had to be safe, and everything had to plug into the support operation that already existed. Flip each card from the challenge to how we solved it.

    07 · The result

    Lower cost, faster replies, same service quality

    Following deployment, the system removed manual triage, cut recurring costs and sped up responses, while keeping replies consistent with support policy.
